Welcome

Newari cuisines are all about the most their culture against popular belief. They have a number of cuisines that are a blend of amazing flavours that are lip-smacking. The cuisine is known for its use of a wide variety of ingredients and spices and for its emphasis on fresh, locally sourced produces. The festivals and the feast are very important to their culture, hence have great food and flavours in their cuisin They are supposed to be healthy and cooked in less fat. The common dishes involve soups, lean curries,vegetables, salads, lean meat, pickles, and curd. The ingredients include tomatoes, chillies, coriander, garlic and mustard oil.m. If you ever land in Kathmandu valley Nepal, you will always be welcomed with a Newari cuisine in hotels and restaurants. The Newari food has been developed over the centuries among the Newars of the valley. Newars being the natives of the valley, they have been cultivating this rich and fertile land for many generations. The food consists of over 200 varieties, each with magical flavours that you cannot find anywhere else. Newari communities are also famous for its elaborate festivals called “Jatras”. These Jatras are never dull with the Newari food and drinks.
