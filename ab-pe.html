Personal Experencies

Newar culture is incredibly diverse and has a unique way of celebrating various stages of life.Newar culture is a unique blend of Hinduism, Buddhism, and their own indigenous beliefs. The customs and traditions related to childhood and young age in Newar culture are quite diverse and interesting. I am one of them ,who have collected beautiful memory since childhood and I am still enjoying them.Every feast,festival and retual are celebrated joyfully with family and friends. Newari cuisine is a harmonious blend of flavors, textures, and aromas, reflecting the cultural diversity and history of Nepal. we pride ourselves on offering a menu that showcases the best of Newari culinary traditions. From the iconic Newari momo, delicately crafted to perfection, to the aromatic spices of our traditional Newari curries, each dish is a testament to our dedication to authenticity.we invite you on a culinary journey through the vibrant and rich tapestry of Newari culture. and celebrate the traditional moment and Newari cuisin,where every dish tells a story of heritage, flavor, and passion.
